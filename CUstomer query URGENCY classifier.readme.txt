Analyze the customer Queries and classify its urgency into high, medium or low priority.
follow the given criteria:
High: Critical issues requiring immediate attention, account hacking, financial loss, security threats, inability to access essential services, or urgent payment problems.
Medium: Issues requiring attention soon but not critical, such as delayed refunds, delivery changes, service disruptions, or problems affecting normal activities.
Low: General inquiries or non-urgent requests, such as asking about subscription plans, updating profile information, or requesting basic product details.

constraint: The query should only be regarding the business, product or transaction.
Input: A customer query in natural Language.
Allowed Output: A single Word high, Medium or Low representing the urgency level.
